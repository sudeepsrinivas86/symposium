ABOUT TECH INNOVA ’ 26- V2.O


Tech Innova ’26 - 2.O, organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indra Ganesan College of Engineering, will be held on 1st OCTOBER, 2026. This technical symposium celebrates innovation, creativity, and modern technology. It includes a mix of technical and non-technical events for students from various institutions. The event provides a platform to showcase skills, learn new ideas, and face real-world challenges. Join us for an exciting and memorable experience that promotes student talent and the future of computing.



ABOUT IGCE


Indra Ganesan College of Engineering, established at 2008 is a leading and organized educational institution is run by Indra Ganesan Educational & Charitable Trust which has consistently followed a growth path owing to our philosophy of constant innovation, up gradation and well defined quality policy. We believe in educating people with the necessary skills to advance the engineering "state of the art".
